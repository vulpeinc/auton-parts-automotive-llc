About Us

Focused on automatic transmissions

Auton parts automotive LLC distributes automotive parts, components and maintenance products to repair shops, distributors, retailers and individual customers.

Our Company

Focused on the products that keep transmissions in service

Auton parts automotive LLC is a limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming. The company specializes in the wholesale and retail distribution of automotive parts, components and maintenance products, with a primary focus on automatic transmission systems.

Our product portfolio includes automatic transmission filters, transmission fluids (ATF and CVT), transmission gaskets, seals, replacement components and related automotive products.

We focus on building long-term business relationships, delivering efficient product solutions, and supporting the maintenance and repair of automatic transmission systems.

Mission

Connecting quality products with customers

To connect quality automotive products with customers through reliability, professionalism and attentive service.

Vision

A trusted name in transmission supply

To be recognized by repair professionals and vehicle owners as a dependable source of automatic transmission parts and maintenance products, built on consistent service and long-term relationships.
