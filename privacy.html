Privacy Policy

How Auton parts automotive LLC handles information collected through this website.

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Auton parts automotive LLC ("the Company", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit this website or contact us. By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Information you provide voluntarily, such as your name, company name, e-mail address, phone number and the content of messages you send us by e-mail.
  • Technical information automatically processed when you visit the website, such as browser type, device information, IP address, pages visited and the date and time of access. This information may be processed by our hosting provider for security and performance purposes.

This website does not include account registration, online payments or checkout functionality.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries, quote requests and other communications;
  • Provide information about our products and operations;
  • Maintain, secure and improve the website;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

This website uses essential browser storage (such as localStorage) to remember your cookie preference. We do not use advertising cookies. Fonts are served from this website, so no third-party font service receives your visit. If analytics or similar tools are added in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

You may accept or reject non-essential cookies through the cookie banner. You can also clear or block cookies and local storage through your browser settings.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers that support our operations, such as website hosting and e-mail providers, under appropriate confidentiality expectations;
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process or governmental request;
  • To protect the rights, property or safety of the Company, our customers or others;
  • In connection with a business transaction such as a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

7. Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided to us, subject to applicable law.

8. Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
